What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list used to organize shopping links, photos, prices and buying notes. Its value comes from helping you compare exact product options. A saved listing is a starting point: confirm the seller, selected variation, measurements and current terms before paying.

This page pairs that research workflow with a small, searchable CAFANS catalog snapshot. Open an individual product reference to review its current listing. If you intend to order using SuperBuy, obtain the original marketplace link and check whether the item can be purchased through your account. A CAFANS reference does not establish agent compatibility, availability or authenticity.

From discovery to a considered order

How to use your SuperBuy spreadsheet shortlist

01

Save the exact option

A useful shortlist identifies a specific variation. Two prices may refer to different sizes, materials or quantities.

  • Record the original marketplace URL and seller.
  • Save color, size, quantity and the date checked.
  • Separate seller claims from confirmed details.
02

Resolve sizing and QC questions

Compare measurements with an item you already own. Keep measurement methods and units consistent.

  • Check the size chart for the selected item.
  • Review warehouse photos for the ordered variation when available.
  • Ask about unclear details and current return terms promptly.
03

Plan the complete parcel

Compare quotes for the same destination, package and service conditions. An item price cannot predict the final delivered cost.

  • Get packed weight and dimensions.
  • Confirm route restrictions and included services.
  • Record freight, fees and destination charges separately.

A little more clarity

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official SuperBuy website?

No. This is an independent product-discovery and comparison guide. SuperBuy is referenced to describe the shopping workflow. The catalog references on this page come from CAFANS.

Where do the SuperBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons go?

Both buttons open https://cafans.com. Individual product cards open the matching CAFANS product listing. You are leaving this guide to review information on that separate website.

Are the displayed prices live checkout prices?

No. They are estimated USD amounts copied from the CAFANS catalog snapshot checked on 19 November 2026. Verify the current price, selected variation and all additional charges before ordering.

Can every product reference be ordered through SuperBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Obtain the original marketplace URL, check it through your SuperBuy account, and confirm current purchasing restrictions and available options before funding an order.

Are product photographs the same as warehouse QC photos?

No. Catalog photos describe the source listing. Warehouse QC photos, when provided, should show the item received for your order. Check size, color, quantity and visible condition, and request specific detail photos when something is unclear. Photos alone do not establish authenticity or material composition.

What should I do if a listing changes or becomes unavailable?

Reopen the source and verify the exact option. Mark the old price or link as outdated in your shortlist. Compare alternatives using the same measurements and cost fields instead of assuming a similar photograph represents the same product.
